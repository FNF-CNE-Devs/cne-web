Displaying Songs

Week Song

Simply add a <song> node to your week xml, containing the song name.

<song>song-name</song>

(if you wanna learn more about week xmls, consider taking a read into it)

Freeplay Exclusive

Or if you prefer not to use weeks, just create your song normally and it'll automatically appear in freeplay.

And optionally, you can make use of ./data/config/freeplaySonglist.txt, it follows a similar format to ./data/weeks/weeks.txt.

song-name
other-song

hot-milk
# This is a comment

It serves to tell Freeplay what songs to show and in what order.

You can add comments to the list by using a hash symbol (#) at the start of the line.
